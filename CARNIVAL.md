# Dead Set: Carnival — rules and game spec (DRAFT)

**Status: design draft for Beta 4, main decisions taken 2026-11-12 (§10). Nothing here is built.**

A bluffing and memory game for **4–12 players**, about 25 minutes, chosen in the lobby next to the other modes. One phone each, plus the TV board as the ballroom.

A masked ball, and Death is a guest. Everyone is dealt a **mask**: one of the familiar Dead Set role cards. You see it once, then the masks are shuffled between players, sometimes for real, sometimes only in pretence, until nobody is sure what they're wearing. Claim a mask to use its power and gain **Souls**. If anyone thinks they're wearing it instead, they can challenge you.

---

## 1. How to win

The game ends **the moment** one of these happens, checked after every action:

1. **A full purse.** A player holds **13 Souls** or more, and wins.
2. **Death's harvest.** A player's **Harvest** reaches **6**. The Harvest counts every Soul that player has reaped through a successful Death claim (§4), whoever wears the Death mask now. That player wins: *Death came for its due.* (Three successful Death claims are enough.)
3. **Ruin.** A player falls to **0 Souls**. The game ends at once, and the **richest** player wins.
   - A tie goes to the higher Harvest.
   - If still tied, those players share the win.

- **If two conditions trigger on the same action** (a Death claim that reaps someone to 0 also completes a Harvest), the player who made the claim wins.
- **Match play:** the winner +3, and every other player +1 for each Soul above 6 they ended on. Scores carry over, and the table returns to the same lobby.

## 2. Setup

1. **Masks:** one per player, from the recommended set for the table size (§6), or a set the host builds in the lobby. **Death is always in.**
2. **Empty chairs:** at **4–5 players**, two extra masks are dealt to two **empty chairs**, the vacant seats from the Empty Table. They join the ball but never act.
3. **Souls:** everyone starts with **6 Souls** from the bank. The **Offering** (the collection plate, centre table) starts empty.
4. **The unmasking:** for 8 seconds every mask is shown face up, on the TV and on every phone, so everyone sees who starts with what. Then they turn face down.
5. **The first dancer:** the lottery wheel picks who goes first. Turns go clockwise.

## 3. A turn

On your turn, do **one** of three things.

### Swap or pretend
- Choose another player or an empty chair. On your phone, secretly **hold Swap** or **hold Pretend**; the two buttons look the same.
- The TV lifts both masks under a cloak, shuffles them and sets them back. Every phone shows the same animation either way.
- **Nobody,** not even the other player, learns whether it was real. You know what you chose, but you still don't see either mask.

### Peek
- Look at your own mask, privately, for 3 seconds.
- The TV announces *"Ada lifts her mask"*; everyone knows you peeked, but not what you saw.

### Claim
1. Name one of the masks in play ("I am the Witch") and tap it on your phone.
2. **Challenges (10 s):** every other player's phone asks *"Is that your mask?"*, with **That's mine!** or **Let it pass**. Everyone must answer (a timeout counts as passing), so hesitation shows on no screen.
3. **No challengers:** you use the mask's power, whether you hold it or not. Nothing is revealed.
4. **Challengers:** you and every challenger reveal your masks on the TV at once.
   - **Whoever really holds the claimed mask** uses its power. If several revealed players hold it (only possible with duplicate Innocents), each does.
   - **Everyone revealed who doesn't hold it** pays **1 Soul** into the Offering.
   - If the claimed mask is on someone who stayed silent, or on an empty chair, every revealed player pays and nobody uses the power.
   - The revealed masks turn face down again, now known to all, until the next shuffles.

**The opening dance:** each player's **first turn must be Swap or pretend** (a setup option makes it the first **two** turns), so the ball is mixed before anyone claims.

**Paying with nothing:** a player who must pay more than they have pays what they have, and Ruin is checked at once.

## 4. The masks

Every mask is an existing Dead Set role card. On a successful claim, the mask's **sting** plays on the TV: its emblem flourishes, with a short sound.

| Mask | Power when claimed successfully |
|---|---|
| **Death** | Take **2 Souls** from one player of your choice. They count toward **your Harvest**. |
| **Conspirator** | Take **3 Souls** from the bank. |
| **Diplomat** | Take **2 Souls** from the bank. |
| **Magistrate** | Take **everything in the Offering**. |
| **Gambler** | The classic **coin toss** plays on the TV. Heads: +3 Souls from the bank. Tails: pay 1 into the Offering. |
| **Ripper** | Take **1 Soul from each neighbour**. |
| **Bard** | You and **both neighbours** each take 1 Soul from the bank. |
| **Guardian** | Take 1 Soul. **Until your next turn**, nobody can take Souls from you (a Death claim, the Ripper or the Jester). A shield shows on your portrait. |
| **Mentalist** | Look at **one other** player's or empty chair's mask, **entirely in private**. You choose on your phone, and nobody learns whose mask you looked at: the TV and every phone show only *"The Mentalist reads someone's mask"*. |
| **Oracle** | Look at **two other** masks (players or empty chairs). **Whose masks are public:** the TV lights up both and draws the Oracle's gaze to them. **What they are stays private**, on the Oracle's phone only. |
| **Witch** | Choose **two other** masks (players or chairs) and secretly swap or pretend, as in §3. Every phone shows the same shuffle. |
| **Double Agent** | Swap your mask **for real** with any other, then look at your new one. |
| **Mimic** | Use the power of **the mask claimed in the turn just before yours**. If there wasn't one, or it was the Mimic, take 1 Soul. |
| **Jester** | Name a player and a mask. The server checks it secretly; the TV says only *right* or *wrong*. Right: that player pays you **2 Souls**. Wrong: you pay 1 into the Offering. |
| **Scapegoat** | Choose a player: they pay **2 Souls** into the Offering. |
| **Innocent** | Take **1 Soul** from the bank. The only mask that can appear twice (11–12 players). |

**Protected players:** the Guardian's shield blocks Death, the Ripper and the Jester's payout. A Death claim on a shielded player reaps nothing.

## 5. Phones, TV and privacy

**Your phone:**
- the ball as a ring of portraits with face-down masks;
- your Souls, your Harvest, and the Offering;
- the three turn buttons;
- the challenge prompt when someone claims;
- pickers for powers.

**The ball diary (memory aid, off by default; a setup option):** a private log of only what **you** have seen, such as *"Turn 4: you peeked: Witch"* or *"Turn 7: Bram revealed the Mentalist"*. It records sightings, never the swaps since; working out where masks went is the game. Tables who want a gentler memory game can switch it on.

**TV:**
- the ballroom backdrop, with portrait medallions around the astrolabe ring and a face-down mask on each;
- the empty chairs as dim medallions;
- every swap animated as two masks rising, crossing under a cloak and settling;
- claims as the mask's emblem over the claimant;
- challenges as the revealed masks flipping together;
- Soul counts as coin pips;
- the Offering as a plate in the centre;
- each player's Harvest as a small scythe tally.

**Privacy rules (as in every Dead Set mode):**
- Swap and Pretend look identical on the phone.
- Every player answers every challenge prompt.
- What a look shows (Peek, the Mentalist, the Oracle, the Double Agent) is always private, with the same timing for everyone. Who was looked at is hidden for the Mentalist and public for the Oracle (§4).
- During any power, every phone shows the same waiting screen.

## 6. Setup (lobby)

**Recommended masks by table size** (Death always included):

| Players | Masks |
|---|---|
| 4–5 | Death, Conspirator, Witch, Mentalist, Magistrate, Gambler (6 masks, with 2 or 1 empty chairs) |
| 6 | the same six |
| 7 | + Ripper |
| 8 | + Guardian |
| 9 | + Double Agent |
| 10 | + Jester |
| 11 | + Bard |
| 12 | + Innocent |

The host can swap in any of the **Diplomat, Oracle, Mimic, Scapegoat or Innocent** instead, keeping one mask per player.

| Setting | Options (default first) |
|---|---|
| Masks | **Recommended**, custom (Death required; only Innocent may repeat) |
| Win target | **13 Souls**; customisable from 8 to 20 |
| Death's harvest | **6**; customisable from 2 to 12 (even numbers, since each reap is 2 Souls), or off |
| Starting Souls | **6** |
| Penalty for a wrong claim | **1 Soul**, 2 |
| Opening dance | **1 forced swap turn each**, 2 |
| Ball diary | **Off**, on |
| Games per match | **1**, 3 |
| Timers | Turn 45 s, challenges 10 s, power picks 20 s, peek 3 s |

**Timeouts:** a turn defaults to **Peek**; a power pick is chosen at random.

Everything is validated per mode (Beta 0).

## 7. End screen

- Every mask turned face up.
- A replay of the whole ball as a timeline: every swap with **real** or **pretend** marked, every claim, challenge and power.
- *"Best liar"*: the most successful claims with the wrong mask.
- *"Sharpest eye"*: the most correct challenges.

## 8. Cast (reused art)

- **The masks** are the existing role cards (Death, Conspirator, Diplomat, Magistrate, Gambler, Ripper, Bard, Guardian, Mentalist, Oracle, Witch, Double Agent, Mimic, Jester, Scapegoat, Innocent), shown as arcana cards with the lattice back.
- **Souls** use the coin emblem, **the Offering** the Gift.
- The Gambler reuses the classic **coin toss**, the Harvest the Death scythe, the empty chairs the Empty Table's medallions.
- **New art:** only a mask frame laid over a card, and the shuffle cloak.

## 9. Practice bots

- Each bot keeps a probability table of where every mask is: it updates on what it has seen and spreads probability across every swap it couldn't verify.
- **Claiming:** a bot claims when it is at least 70% sure of its mask, or bluffs a strong mask with a small chance.
- **Challenging:** a bot challenges when it is at least 60% sure it holds the claimed mask.
- **Swapping:** a bot pretends or swaps at random, and swaps for real away from a mask it knows is weak.

Simulations tune the targets and the penalty, so a 6-player game lasts about 20–25 minutes and Death's harvest wins about one game in five.

## 10. Decided (2026-11-12)

- **The Mentalist** looks in complete private: nobody learns whose mask was read.
- **The Oracle** looks at two masks: whose is public, what they are is private.
- **Targets:** 13 Souls and a Harvest of 6 by default, both customisable in setup.
- **The opening dance:** one forced Swap-or-pretend turn each by default, two as a setup option.
- **The ball diary:** off by default, on as a setup option.

## 11. Still to decide

1. **Should empty chairs ever take part at 6 or more players?** This draft says no.
2. **Should the Guardian's shield block a Scapegoat payment?** This draft says no; the Scapegoat pays into the Offering, not to a player.
