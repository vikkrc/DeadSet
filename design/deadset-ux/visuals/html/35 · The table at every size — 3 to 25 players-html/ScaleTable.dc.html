The TV always shows every name: a 760 px table with 58 px medallions even at 25; two rings from 13; the ledger splits into two columns from 16; threads thin to 1.5 px past 12.

The seal scales too: dots up to 12; from 13 they become small lozenges on the rim (bead, sealed and you still read at a glance); the centre always shows the count or whose turn.

Never scroll, never pinch the table. Above 18 the lens replaces tiny targets. Lists (the Gazette, the ledger) are where long names live.
