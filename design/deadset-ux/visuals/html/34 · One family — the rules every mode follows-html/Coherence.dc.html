One family: rules every mode follows

A mode earns its own object and accent. Everything else is shared.

The accent tints the cover window, the backdrop glow and the table's rim glow. Buttons, text and frames stay gilt, bone, vellum and wax in every mode.

Light

Three lights, chosen by the story

Candle · dayMoon · nightBlood moon

Candle is the default. Moon (silver gilt) for anything that happens while others sleep: Classic nights, Midnight, the Moon crew. Blood moon only for its rule.

Type

Blackletter names; Garamond speaks

The SalesmanBuy the whole caseSmall caps for labels

Blackletter for titles, headlines, names of things and big numbers. Garamond for every instruction, answer and button. Never blackletter under 20 px.

Commitment

Bone taps, wax holds

Bone (tap) for a private choice nobody sees you make. Wax (hold 0.6 s) for anything public or final. Ghost buttons for “skip” and “back”.

Vellum: cards, slips, the Gazette, story and dilemma cards. Ink with gilt frames: the world, the table, sheets. Nothing else (no grey cards, no white panels).

Pictures

One etching set for every object

Pencil etchings in a sunburst roundel for things; engraved emblems for roles and modes. No emoji, no flat UI icons outside the tray and header.

The table

One table, three forms

Seating, playing, seal (board 26). The ornate astrolabe in every mode; a mode may put things on it, never restyle it.
