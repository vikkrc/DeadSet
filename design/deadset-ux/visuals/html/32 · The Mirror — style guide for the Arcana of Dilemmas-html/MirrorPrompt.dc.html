The Arcana of Dilemmas, for The Mirror only. Text-first: any number of dilemmas and answers can be added without new art.

2The fates (answers)

Three text-only cards, numerals I · II · III in a small ring. No icons: an answer is its words. Garamond 500, 16.5 px on phones (26 px on TV), balanced, centred; the card grows to fit three lines.

3Seals and the glass

VA

Your seal: red wax, your initial. Their seal: silver, their initial. 34 px on phones, 56 px on TV. One segmented switch chooses which seal you hold.

VYouAshA

The glass: a silvered band above the card, always reading left (you) → right (who you read).

4Colour and type

Silver (#6f8aa8) is the Mirror accent: the glass, the sigil ring, their seal, the backdrop glow. Never on buttons.

5Writing dilemmas

  • Question ≤ 110 characters, one sentence, second person or a scene. No real people, no real sensitive topics.
  • Answers ≤ 28 characters, start with a verb, same grammar across the three, each plausible.
  • Title “The …”, names the situation, not an answer.
  • Data: { id, pack, title, prompt, choices[3] }; translations add the title as a fifth field.

6Motion

  • Deal: the card turns face up on TV and phones together (Turn, 700 ms). Seal: wax drops (Stamp, 300 ms). Reveal: a held breath of 0.6 s, then the three fates turn I → III, 150 ms apart; their seal glows last.
Optional, later

A sigil per pack (Core: the hand mirror; future packs: their own emblem) instead of per dilemma. Adding a dilemma never needs new art.

Do

Let the question be the biggest, darkest text on screen · keep answers short and parallel · keep the card flat behind the text · use silver only for the Mirror's own parts.

Don't

Italic or blackletter questions · icons on answers · textured or tinted paper behind text · A/B/C form rows · put this card style in other modes (it is the Mirror's own).
