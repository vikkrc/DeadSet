Coffins: the opening

Every coffin is opened the same way: chosen at its owner’s grave, lifted, held, turned. The face shows only when the turn completes.

  • Coffin cards are arcana: lattice back with a cross sigil face down; colour-coded faces readable at 48 px: gilt Gold with a large coin, oxblood Curse with the cracked-heart seal from the icon set, black-and-silver Death; each names itself in small caps. Same size and brightness face down, so a glance gives nothing away.
  • Ownership is spatial: each grave sits on its owner’s spoke of the table, with their initial on the top card. A lifted card leaves a ghost outline on its grave until it lands.
  • No early reveal: the face is sent with a reveal time and rendered only after the turn. Phones and TV turn together.
  • Rhythm for long digs: after the first two, opens shorten to 1.2 s (the held breath stays). The last coffin of a bid always gets the full sequence.
