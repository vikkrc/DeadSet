The Omen: the reveal waits for the needle

Rule for every spin in Dead Set: the result is shown only after the needle has stopped.

  • Nothing leaks early. During the spin no face is lit, no sound hints, the ledger and phones show “The wheel turns…”. The result isn’t even in the page until the reveal (the server sends it with a reveal time).
  • Phones mirror the TV with the same veil and the same stop, then reveal together; no phone may run ahead.
  • The same rule for every spin: the Missions lottery hand, the wheel draws (Exorcism, Carnival, Séance, Coffins), the Graverobbers coin and Midnight’s dawn turn all reveal after the motion ends, with the 0.8 s held breath.
  • Reduced motion: the needle jumps to the stop, the hold and the reveal stay.
