The key surface owns the stage band. It is the largest thing on the phone and the hero on the TV; everything else is smaller.

Where it is an object, the seal sits top right of the stage, 104 px, always in the same spot, so who-has-acted is answered at a glance in every mode.

Choosing a player is always on the table. Any step that targets a person (accuse, vote, swap, ask, dig a player's plot) brings the playing table back.
