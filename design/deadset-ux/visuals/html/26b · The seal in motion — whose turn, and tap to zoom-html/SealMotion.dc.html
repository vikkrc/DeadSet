The seal in motion

The small table always says whose turn it is, and always opens the big one.

Whose turn: the centre flips
  • Every turn change runs 2 → 3 → 4: the bead moves to the seat, the centre flips to that player, then back.
  • Your turn stops at 3 on your own medallion, with a slow gold pulse and the herald slip (board 28).
  • No turns (everyone acts at once): the centre stays on the round info and each seat's dot turns gilt as it seals.
  • Reduced motion: a cross-fade instead of the flip.
