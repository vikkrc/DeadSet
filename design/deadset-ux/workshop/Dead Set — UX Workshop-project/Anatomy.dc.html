Every phone screen, every mode

Five bands, always in this order

1HeaderThe unit and number (Day II), the step, the alive count, the menu. Unchanged from today.
2Move strip · newOne line that says what you can do now and how long you have. The dot glows when it is your move, stays dim when you are waiting ("Cass is choosing a sign"). It replaces the kicker-plus-italic-paragraph instructions that differ per mode, and the duplicate timers.
3StageThe ring, the ball, the cemetery, the cards: the mode's own picture. It gets all the space that is left; nothing else may scroll it off screen.
4Tray · newFour fixed slots with icons and labels: My card, The board, Notes, Story. Absent slots stay in place, dimmed, so muscle memory works across games. A badge counts unread notes. Replaces today's chip rows that wrap to two lines.
5DockThe one action, full width. Secret choices are bone (tap), public acts are wax (hold). The host's advance moves into the room menu during play, so it never competes with the player's own action.
Sizes on a 390 × 844 phone

Header 64 · move strip 56 · tray 52 · dock 76. The stage gets about 590 px, which is enough for a 300 px ring and one line of slips without scrolling.
