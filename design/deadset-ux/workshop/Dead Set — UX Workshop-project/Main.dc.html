From consistent to effortless

The consistency pass landed: no phone text under 11 px, TV text readable, every tap target 44 px, one card, one commit grammar. What is left is pacing, guidance and delight.

This canvas works through the biggest changes. Each board is numbered; the implementation guide refers to the same numbers.

I

Three scene sizes, not one

Full-screen scenes are for moments that change the game. Turn-level events (a swap, a sign, a dig) become a banner over the board or play on the ring itself. Boards 10, 13.

II

One move strip

Every phone screen answers "what do I do now?" in one line, in one place, with the time left. Board 5.

III

The tray

My card · The board · Notes · Story: four fixed, labelled slots instead of chips that wrap. Board 5.

IV

A lobby you can see at once

The host sets up in three tabs with recommended defaults; guests get one screen. Boards 3, 4, 11.

V

One TV grid

Header strip, stage, ledger, caption: every mode fills the same frame, so no half-empty screens. Board 9.

VI

Every game has a face

Eleven covers with eleven different emblems, used on the home page, the lobby, the rule book and the end screen. Boards 1, 2.

VII

Endings people talk about

Awards drawn from the match, a ledger for the whole evening, and one tap to the next game. Boards 8, 12.
