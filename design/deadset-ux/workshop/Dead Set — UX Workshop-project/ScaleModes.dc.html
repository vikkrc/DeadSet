The rule

Things that belong to a player live on their seat. When there is room (≤ 6–8) they sit beside it; when it is crowded they fold into a tab on the outer edge of the medallion (right edge for seats on the right half, left edge on the left), clear of the name plate, and the chosen one unfolds in the context row.

The other modes at 12

The Omen: 12 seers on the wheel rim, dots become lozenges.
Graverobbers: three crews by seat thirds; the seal shows crews as arcs, not dots.
The Mirror: the glass still shows one pair; the TV draws 12 arrows, thinned, and lights each pair in turn at the reveal.
Mimic: the lantern passes on a 44 px table; the places board is unchanged.
