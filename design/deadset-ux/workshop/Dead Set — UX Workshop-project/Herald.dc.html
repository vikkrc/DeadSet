TV: the caption band (bottom 76 px of the grid) is the herald. News crosses it as a ribbon in the rung's colour, holds, then becomes the plain caption again. Never over the table or the ledger.
1The herald slipDrops 8 px over the move strip, same width, 56 px tall. Holds 2.5 s (4 s for deaths), then folds up into the strip.
2The header keeps itThe latest herald line replaces the header subtitle for 10 s, in italics, so a glance still catches it.
3Private newsA wax dot on the tray slot it belongs to. Nothing private ever reaches the TV or the herald text.
4Never on the stageThe table, ball or wheel is not covered. Taps there keep working while a slip is shown.
  • One at a time. A newer slip replaces after 1.2 s minimum; a queue of three collapses to “3 new · see the Gazette”.
  • Swipe up dismisses; tap opens the Gazette at that line.
  • Quiet hours: during discussion only deaths and the 10-second warning may drop.
  • System news (TV offline, reconnecting) is a small chip in the header, not a slip.
  • Full scenes stay for deaths, reveals and the end only.
