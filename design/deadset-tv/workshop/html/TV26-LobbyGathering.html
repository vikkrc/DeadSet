1The table takes overOnce everyone expected is seated (or 60 s pass with no joins), the join panel folds into the band’s corner and the table grows to 680 px. Late joiners still have the code and QR there.
2Ready, from the sofaA gilt tick on every ready seat, a dashed ring on the rest, and the rim fills with readiness. The disc counts in 80 px. The band names who we are waiting for once 3 or fewer remain.
3Moods, not padlocksSmall tags above a seat say what that player is doing: “choosing a face”, “reading the rules”. They replace today’s 14 px lock chips.
4The teach loopThe Now card is the teach step (cycling every 8 s, dot track). The steps are each game’s four from the guide; Rule cards and the story setting add a step of their own.
5The deck, readableTonight’s deck as 64 px cards with labels under them, never breaking mid-word (keep-all; shorten in content if needed). Counts in gilt. It reshuffles live as players join or the host changes the deck.
6Rules in forceEach active Rule card with its one-line effect, in 22 px. When the host adds or removes one, the card deals in or folds out (Deal / Fold).
