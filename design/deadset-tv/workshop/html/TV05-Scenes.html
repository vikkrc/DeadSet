1One centred stageEvery scene composes on the same 1200 × 620 safe area, centred: kicker 22 px, title 92 px, the object row, one line of 32 px. Nothing is left-aligned or off-centre (today’s ballot box and front page are).
2The people, largeA scene about a person shows their medallion at 2× with their name. Today’s scenes are text-only; the faces are what the room reacts to.
3The object turnsThe thing revealed is a real card that turns (Turn verb, 700 ms) after a 0.6 s held breath; then the stamp lands (Seal). The result is never in the DOM before its reveal time.
4Header and band stayScenes keep the proscenium, the header and the band. The band shows the scene’s name and a thin progress line instead of the herald, so the room knows it will end.
5No blank framesScenes queue; the next arch opens from the last frame of the one before. Exorcism’s end currently shows an empty red frame for ~3 s (board 0).
6Scene sizes from Part IFull: deaths, reveals, verdicts, the end. Banner: the band ribbon. Inline: on the table. This board is the Full template for all eleven games.
