1The evening, not just the gameAfter the third game (or when the host taps “How are we doing?”), the TV shows the whole evening: the champion, every game with its winners, and the running ledger. The data is “Tonight so far” from the lobby (board 28).
2Every game, in orderEach game played as its cover with the winners’ faces under it, in the order played. A rematch shows as one cover with a ×2.
3The championThe evening’s top scorer at 200 px with one line that earned it. Ties share the title.
4The share cardTonight’s front page as a vellum card (share.js drawEveningCard), with a QR so anyone can save it. Names can be anonymised from the host’s phone.
5Honours, one at a timeEvery honour earned tonight (from each game’s awards: Red herring, Hand on the wheel, Called it…) takes the panel in turn, 5 s each: its etching, title, holder with portrait, the game it came from and the line that earned it; a dot track and the next one waiting underneath.
6Goodnight“Goodnight” fades the table out with the candles, leaving the front page on screen for a minute before the idle loop.
