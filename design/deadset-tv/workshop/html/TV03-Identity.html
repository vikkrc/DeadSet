Four signature motifs, used the same way in every game

The arch

Every phase change opens through the Codex arch (the card window shape). Ours alone; replaces cross-fades.

The candle

The one idle motion: a wick in the disc that shortens with the clock and gutters at zero.

The seal

Every decision lands as wax: a stamp, a 30 ms buzz on phones, a low thud on TV.

The page

Records arrive as vellum that unfolds: the Gazette, the story cards, the dilemma.
