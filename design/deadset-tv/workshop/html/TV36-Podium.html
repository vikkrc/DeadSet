1A podium for points gamesThe Omen, the Mirror, Carnival, Coffins and Graverobbers (by crew) end on a podium: the top three as portraits on vellum plinths with their roman place and points, the winner tallest.
2The raceUnder the podium, a quiet line chart of the top three’s points round by round, so the room can relive the swing (“you were ahead until round VI”). Gilt for the winner, two lighter tones for the rest; the names label the line ends.
3Everyone else, readableThe ledger lists every score at 24/28 px and the game’s honours with their etchings (awards.js: Hand on the wheel, Steady hand, Mind reader…).
4TeamsGraverobbers and Missions show two plinths (the crews or sides), each with its members’ faces, instead of three.
5Then the eveningPoints feed the evening ledger; after the third game the band offers the evening screen (board 37).
6OrderThe peak (board 35) plays first; the podium rises plinth by plinth (III, II, then I with a bell) as it settles.
