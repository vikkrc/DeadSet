Every game’s finale

The same peak, the game’s own objects. Today every game ends on a title and one line.

Proposal
Twelve finales
1One beat, eleven gamesEvery finale uses the peak from boards 33–34: rays and gilt dust for a win the room shares, mist and embers for a dark one. Only the objects change.
2The game’s object, etchedEach finale puts the game’s own etching on a vellum medallion beside the winner (wheel, ball, coin, sun, mask, Ghost), never a flat shape.
3Then the resultsAfter 6 s the peak settles into the reckoning (board 9) for team games or the podium (board 36) for points games.
